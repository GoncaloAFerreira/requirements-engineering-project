## 2. Candidate Requirements

From elicitation.md we have made four candidate already which are

The system shall - 
1. Prevent overbookings.
2. Allow accessibility for those with impairment
3. Allow everyone to book equipment without direct department interaction
4. The system shall support up to 300 users monthly and 15 users daily.

These are candidate requirements made in the previous findings but they still require further analysis.

1. Cr-01 Needs more information about what makes a conflicting booking.
2. Cr-02 Is too vague because accessibility is a to broad statement
3. Cr-04 need more evidence to justify the numbers
