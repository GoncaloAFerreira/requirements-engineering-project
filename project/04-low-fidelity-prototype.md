# 4 Low-fidelity prototype


## 4.1 Journey and purpose

Identify the user journey and requirements your prototype explores. Explain what you want to investigate.

The user journey I chose for this prototype is making a facility booking (UC01). I chose this specific journey because it is the main functionality of the proposed system and the journey will address the booking difficulties that was shown in Scenario C

The purpose of this low-fidelity prototype is to understand how users can navigate the system and make bookings without much difficulty, this will also help me identify the clarity of facility information, the availability, ease of navigation and getting booking feedback and it will also help me find missing requirements based on what the prototype looks like and what it needs.
